# `randomnessPid` in the SQLite amalgamation is read in `unixOpen` and written both there and in
# `unixRandomness` without synchronization. SQLite documents the race as harmless right at the
# access ("two or more threads all trying to open databases at the same instant might all reset
# the PRNG. But multiple resets are harmless."), and the writer in `unixRandomness` runs under
# SQLite's own PRNG mutex, which the reader in `unixOpen` does not take. Two concurrent SQLite
# queries, one opening a database and one writing, are enough to report it.
fun:*unixOpen*
