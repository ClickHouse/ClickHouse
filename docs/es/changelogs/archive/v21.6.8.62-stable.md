<div id="bug-fix">
  #### Corrección de errores
</div>

* Retroportado en [#26194](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26194): Corrige `sharding_key` a partir de una columna sin función para `remote()` (antes, `select * from remote('127.1', system.one, dummy)` provocaba el error `Unknown column: dummy, there are only columns .`). [#25824](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/25824) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Retroportado en [#26108](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26108): Corrige un fallo poco frecuente del servidor debido a `abort` en el client de ZooKeeper. Corrige [#25813](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/25813). [#26079](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26079) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado en [#26167](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26167): Corrige `joinGet` con columnas LowCarinality, cierra [#25993](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/25993). [#26118](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26118) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* Retroportado en [#26205](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26205): Corrige un posible fallo si se usa más de una expresión `untuple`. [#26179](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26179) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Retroportado en [#26227](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26227): Elimina un salto de línea adicional en la columna `thread_name` de la tabla `system.stack_trace`. Esto corrige [#24124](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/24124). [#26210](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26210) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
