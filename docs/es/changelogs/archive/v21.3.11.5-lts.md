<div id="improvement">
  #### Mejora
</div>

* retroportado en [#24085](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/24085): Se admite especificar el esquema de la tabla para la fuente del diccionario de PostgreSQL. Cierra [#23958](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23958). [#23980](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23980) ([Kseniia Sumarokova](https://github.com/kssenii)).

<div id="bug-fix">
  #### Corrección de errores
</div>

* Retroportado en [#24002](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/24002): Soluciona SIGSEGV en `GROUP BY` externo y en la fila de overflow (es decir, consultas como `SELECT FROM GROUP BY WITH TOTALS SETTINGS max_bytes_before_external_group_by>0, max_rows_to_group_by>0, group_by_overflow_mode='any', totals_mode='before_having'`). [#23962](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23962) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Retroportado en [#24032](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/24032): Soluciona un fallo en MergeJoin, cierra [#24010](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/24010). [#24013](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/24013) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
