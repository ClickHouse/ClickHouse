[#65170](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/65170) ([Nikita Mikhaylov](https://github.com/nikitamikhaylov)).
* نُقل إلى الفرع الأقدم [#65892](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/65892): مراعاة حد CPU الخاص بـ cgroup في Keeper. [#65819](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/65819) ([Antonio Andelic](https://github.com/antonio2368)).

[#65632](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/65632) ([Nikita Taranov](https://github.com/nickitat)).
