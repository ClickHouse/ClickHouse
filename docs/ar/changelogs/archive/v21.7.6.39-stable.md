[#26900](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26900) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).

PARTITION ID xxx` يُتحقَّق الآن من صحته. يُصلح [#25718](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/25718). [#26963](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26963) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* نُقل إلى الفرع الأقدم في [#27050](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27050): [RFC] إصلاح احتمال تكدس mutation بسبب حالة سباق مع DROP&#95;RANGE. [#27002](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/27002) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).

[#27064](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/27064) ([Vitaly Baranov](https://github.com/vitlibar)).
