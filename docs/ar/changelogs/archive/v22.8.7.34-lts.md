([https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/NEWS](https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/NEWS)). يُغلق هذا [#42252](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/42252). [#42327](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/42327) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
