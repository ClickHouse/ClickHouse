## Migrating to ClickHouse Observability from Elastic {#migrating-to-clickstack-from-elastic-self-configured}

This guide is intended for users migrating from the Elastic Stack — specifically those using Kibana to monitor logs, traces, and metrics collected via Elastic Agent and stored in Elasticsearch. It focuses on fully managed ClickHouse Observability and covers search queries, data collection, and application instrumentation. Self-managed instructions are included where the steps differ.

Fully managed ClickHouse Observability handles schemas, storage, and scaling. You don't need to map Elasticsearch internals to ClickHouse to get started. For self-managed deployments, see [equivalent concepts](/clickstack/self-configured/migration/elastic/concepts) and [type mappings](/clickstack/self-configured/migration/elastic/types).

Before beginning a migration, it's important to understand the tradeoffs between ClickHouse Observability and the Elastic Stack.

You should consider moving to ClickHouse Observability if:

- You're ingesting large volumes of observability data and want to evaluate alternatives to your current storage and compute costs.
- You experience poor search performance at scale or face ingestion bottlenecks.
- You want to correlate observability signals with business data using SQL, unifying observability and analytics workflows.
- You're committed to OpenTelemetry and want to avoid vendor lock-in.
- You want to take advantage of the separation of storage and compute in ClickHouse Cloud.

However, ClickHouse Observability may not be suitable if:

- You use observability data primarily for security use cases and need a SIEM-focused product.
- Universal profiling is a critical part of your workflow.
- You require a business intelligence (BI) dashboarding platform. ClickHouse Observability intentionally has opinionated visual workflows for SREs and developers and isn't designed as a Business Intelligence (BI) tool. For equivalent capabilities, we recommend using [Grafana with the ClickHouse plugin](/integrations/connectors/data-visualization/grafana/index) or [Superset](/integrations/connectors/data-visualization/superset-and-clickhouse).
