CC: @tavplubix. [#17944](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17944) ([Winter Zhang](https://github.com/zhang2014)).
* Бэкпортировано в [#18080](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/18080): исправлено `std::out_of_range: basic_string` при парсинге URL S3. [#18059](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/18059) ([Vladimir Chebotarev](https://github.com/excitoon)).
