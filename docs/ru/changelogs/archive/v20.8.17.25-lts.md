[#22669](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22669) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).

Исправляет [#22438](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22438). [#22684](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22684) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
