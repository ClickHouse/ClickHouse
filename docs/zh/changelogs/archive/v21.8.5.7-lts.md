<div id="bug-fix">
  #### 缺陷修复
</div>

* 已在 [#28033](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28033) 中回移：修复了 windowFunnel 的 &quot;strict&quot; 模式缺陷。此修复解决了 [#27469](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27469)。[#27563](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/27563) ([achimbab](https://github.com/achimbab)).
* 已在 [#27782](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27782) 中回移：修复了子查询中使用 union distinct 时的列过滤问题。关闭 [#27578](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27578)。[#27689](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/27689) ([Kseniia Sumarokova](https://github.com/kssenii)).
* 已在 [#27867](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27867) 中回移：防止来自 Kafka 的 NULL (墓碑) 消息到达时，某些格式发生崩溃。关闭 [#19255](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/19255)。[#27794](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/27794) ([filimonov](https://github.com/filimonov)).
* 已在 [#28346](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28346) 中回移：修复了 `DROP PART` 中一个罕见缺陷，该缺陷可能导致报错 `Unexpected merged part intersects drop range`。[#27807](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/27807) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* 已在 [#27855](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27855) 中回移：修复了几个可能导致副本之间出现分歧的缺陷。[#27808](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/27808) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
* 已在 [#27958](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27958) 中回移：修复了对类型为 `LowCardinality(UUID)` 的列执行带 extremes 的选择时的问题。[#27918](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/27918) ([Vitaly Baranov](https://github.com/vitlibar)).
* 已在 [#27953](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27953) 中回移：在创建 Distributed 表之前先检查集群名称，不允许使用错误的集群名称创建表。修复了 [#27832](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27832)。[#27927](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/27927) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
* 已在 [#28209](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28209) 中回移：修复了读取缓冲区因“attempt to read after end of file”而失败的情况。关闭 [#26149](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26149)。[#28150](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/28150) ([Filatenkov Artur](https://github.com/FArthur-cmd)).

<div id="bug-fix-user-visible-misbehaviour-in-official-stable-release">
  #### 缺陷修复 (官方稳定版本中用户可见的异常行为)
</div>

* 已在 [#27974](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27974) 中回移：修复了 `JSONExtract` 函数对 `Nullable(String)` 类型 NULL 值的处理问题。此修复解决了 [#27929](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27929) 和 [#27930](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27930)。该问题最初由 [https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/25452](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/25452) 引入。[#27939](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/27939) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).
* 已在 [#28117](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28117) 中回移：修复了因 context/config reloader 关闭顺序不正确而导致的极罕见关闭时 segfault。[#28088](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/28088) ([nvartolomei](https://github.com/nvartolomei)).
* 已在 [#28180](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28180) 中回移：修复了从 `WHERE` 移动到 `PREWHERE` 的条件数量可能过多的问题 (该优化由设置 `optimize_move_to_prewhere` 控制) 。[#28139](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/28139) ([lthaooo](https://github.com/lthaooo)).
* 已在 [#28254](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28254) 中回移：修复了 `clickhouse-keeper` 中的错误行为：对子节点执行 `set` request 时会触发列表 watches (`getChildren`) 。[#28190](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/28190) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* 已在 [#28342](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28342) 中回移：修复了 `clickhouse-keeper` 中一个罕见的 bug，即 client 可能会在收到 request-response 之前先收到 watch response。[#28197](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/28197) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* 已在 [#28263](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28263) 中回移：修复了带有 `Nullable(LowCardinality)` 类型和 extremes 的 queries 可能读取未初始化内存的问题。修复了 [#28165](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28165)。[#28205](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/28205) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
* 已在 [#28292](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28292) 中回移：修复了在启用设置 `optimize_read_in_order` 时，带有 `ORDER BY` 的 `Merge` 表 queries 结果不一致的问题。[#28266](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/28266) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
* 已在 [#28402](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/28402) 中回移：修复了因新 part 被替换为空 part 而导致的 intersecting parts 问题。[#28310](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/28310) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
