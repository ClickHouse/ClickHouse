<div id="bug-fix-user-visible-misbehaviour-in-official-stable-release">
  #### 缺陷修复 (官方稳定版本中用户可见的异常行为)
</div>

* 已在 [#36244](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/36244) 中回移：修复配额与异步插入配合使用时的问题。[#35645](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/35645) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
* 已在 [#36240](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/36240) 中回移：修复 `Object` 类型中子列可能丢失的问题。[#35682](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/35682) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
* 已在 [#36242](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/36242) 中回移：修复读取 `Object` 类型的子列时，可能出现 `Can't adjust last granule` 异常的问题。[#35687](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/35687) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
* 已在 [#36147](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/36147) 中回移：修复在 `kafka_num_consumers > 1` 且 `kafka_thread_per_consumer = 0` 时，从 `Kafka` 表读取数据的问题。恢复并行和多线程读取能力，该功能在 21.11 中曾被意外破坏。关闭 [#35153](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/35153)。[#35973](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/35973) ([filimonov](https://github.com/filimonov)).
* 已在 [#36276](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/36276) 中回移：修复按逆序读取空数组时的问题 (出现在按主键前缀降序排序的查询中) 。[#36215](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/36215) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
