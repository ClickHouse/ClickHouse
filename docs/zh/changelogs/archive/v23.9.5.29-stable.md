<div id="bug-fix-user-visible-misbehavior-in-an-official-stable-release">
  #### 缺陷修复 (官方稳定版本中用户可见的异常行为)
</div>

* 修复表基于表函数时无法从系统表查询的问题。[#55540](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/55540) ([MikhailBurdukov](https://github.com/MikhailBurdukov)).
* 修复 `view()` 函数中 UNION 的查询结果不完整的问题。[#56274](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/56274) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
* 修复添加 Object(JSON) 类型列时发生崩溃的问题 [#56307](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/56307) ([Nikita Mikhaylov](https://github.com/nikitamikhaylov)).
* 修复 Kerberos 初始化期间发生段错误的问题 [#56401](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/56401) ([Nikolay Degterinsky](https://github.com/evillique)).
* 修复 RabbitMQ OpenSSL 动态加载问题 [#56703](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/56703) ([Igor Nikonov](https://github.com/devcrafter)).
* 修复数据中存在零值时 GCD codec 发生崩溃的问题 [#56704](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/56704) ([Nikita Mikhaylov](https://github.com/nikitamikhaylov)).
* 修复 FPC codec 中的崩溃问题 [#56795](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/56795) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
