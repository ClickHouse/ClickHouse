* 已在 [#60837](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/60837) 中回移：修复在 INSERT SELECT JOIN 中使用 input() 时发生的崩溃问题。关闭 [#60035](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/60035)。[#60765](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/60765) ([Kruglov Pavel](https://github.com/Avogar)).
* 已在 [#60911](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/60911) 中回移：避免从 S3 读取时因跳过过多键而导致 segfault。[#60849](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/60849) ([Antonio Andelic](https://github.com/antonio2368)).
