<div id="bug-fix-user-visible-misbehaviour-in-official-stable-release">
  #### 缺陷修复 (官方稳定版本中用户可见的异常行为)
</div>

* 已在 [#29121](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/29121) 中回移：改进了对连接是否可用的检查，并在 RabbitMQ 关闭时额外捕获所有异常，以防万一。[#28797](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/28797) ([Kseniia Sumarokova](https://github.com/kssenii)).
* 已在 [#29027](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/29027) 中回移：修复 `GLOBAL IN` 子查询使用的线程数问题 (自 [#19414](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/19414) 的缺陷修复后，它一直以单线程执行) 。[#28997](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/28997) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
* 已在 [#29193](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/29193) 中回移：修复在 Avro 输入格式中向类型为 LowCardinality(Nullable) 的列插入数据时发生段错误的问题。[#29132](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/29132) ([Kruglov Pavel](https://github.com/Avogar)).

* 合并 [#27963](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/27963) [#29063](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/29063) ([Maksim Kita](https://github.com/kitaisreal)).
* 修复未捕获异常导致终止的问题 [#29216](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/29216) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
