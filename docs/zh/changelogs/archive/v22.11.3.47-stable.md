* 已在 [#44113](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/44113) 中回移：在构建步骤中恢复 sha512 校验和。[#44017](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/44017) ([Mikhail f. Shiryaev](https://github.com/Felixoid)) 。
