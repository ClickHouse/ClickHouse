<div id="bug-fix">
  #### 缺陷修复
</div>

* 已在 [#26191](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26191) 中回移：修复 `remote()` 中 `sharding_key` 取自不带函数的列时的问题 (此前 `select * from remote('127.1', system.one, dummy)` 会报错：`Unknown column: dummy, there are only columns .`) 。[#25824](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/25824) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* 已在 [#26109](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26109) 中回移：修复 ZooKeeper 客户端中因 `abort` 导致的罕见服务器崩溃问题。修复了 [#25813](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/25813)。[#26079](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26079) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* 已在 [#26142](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26142) 中回移：修复在关闭 `validate_polygons` 设置时，`pointInPolygon` 可能发生崩溃的问题。[#26113](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26113) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* 已在 [#26170](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26170) 中回移：修复 `joinGet` 在 LowCardinality 列上的问题，关闭 [#25993](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/25993)。[#26118](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26118) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* 已在 [#26206](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26206) 中回移：修复使用多个 `untuple` 表达式时可能发生崩溃的问题。[#26179](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26179) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* 已在 [#26229](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26229) 中回移：移除 `system.stack_trace` 表中 `thread_name` 列里的多余换行符。此修复解决了 [#24124](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/24124)。[#26210](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26210) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* 修复遍历不存在的远程目录时抛出异常的问题。[#26296](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26296) ([ianton-ru](https://github.com/ianton-ru)).
