  # 2022 更新日志
</div>

<div id="clickhouse-release-v213983-lts-fixme-as-compared-to-v213876-lts">
  ### ClickHouse 版本 v21.3.9.83-lts FIXME (相较于 v21.3.8.76-lts)
</div>

<div id="improvement">
  #### 改进
</div>

* 已在 [#23680](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23680) 中回移：修复了 `quantile(s)TDigest`，并根据 tdunning/t-digest 3.2+ 新增了对单例质心的特殊处理。同时还修复了该算法早期版本实现中质心过度压缩的问题。[#23314](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23314) ([Vladimir Chebotarev](https://github.com/excitoon)) 。

<div id="bug-fix">
  #### 缺陷修复
</div>

* 已在 [#23579](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23579)中回移：修复了后台清理旧块时一个极少出现的竞态条件。如果某个块过于接近去重窗口的末尾，可能会导致该块未被去重。[#23301](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23301) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
* 已在 [#23677](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23677)中回移：在分区裁剪期间不要放宽 NOT 条件。此修复解决了 [#23305](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23305) 和 [#21539](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21539)。[#23310](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23310) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).
* 已在 [#23582](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23582)中回移：* 修复了 `join_algorithm = 'auto'` 时 dict join 中的缺陷。关闭 [#23002](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23002)。[#23312](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23312) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* 已在 [#23585](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23585)中回移：允许像 21.1 版本之前那样将更多条件移动到 `PREWHERE`。移动的条件数量不足可能会导致性能变差。[#23397](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23397) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
* 已在 [#23592](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23592)中回移：修复了在使用多磁盘配置创建 ReplicatedMergeTree 表失败时出现的 `Cannot unlink file` 错误。此修复关闭了 [#21755](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21755)。[#23433](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23433) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
* 已在 [#23590](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23590)中回移：修复了 `ReplacingMergeTree` 垂直合并中的边界场景问题。在极少数情况下，这可能导致合并失败，并出现类似 `Incomplete granules are not allowed while blocks are granules size` 的异常。[#23459](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23459) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
* 已在 [#23612](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23612)中回移：修复了 restart / stop 命令卡住的问题。关闭 [#20214](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/20214)。[#23552](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23552) ([filimonov](https://github.com/filimonov)).
* 已在 [#23696](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23696)中回移：在聚合函数 `mannWhitneyUTest` 的两个样本中的值完全相同时，新增了抛出异常的处理。此修复解决了 [#23646](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23646)。[#23654](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23654) ([Nikita Mikhaylov](https://github.com/nikitamikhaylov)).
