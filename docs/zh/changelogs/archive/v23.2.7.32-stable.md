<div id="bug-fix-user-visible-misbehavior-in-an-official-stable-release">
  #### 缺陷修复 (官方稳定版本中用户可见的异常行为)
</div>

* 修复含多个 StorageJoin 的查询中出现键未找到错误的问题 [#49137](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/49137) ([vdimir](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* 修复过期分区片段加载时的竞态条件 [#49223](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/49223) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
* 修复 DISTINCT 中的缺陷 [#49628](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/49628) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* 修复 randomStringUTF8(<uneven number>) 中的 msan 问题 [#49750](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/49750) ([Robert Schulze](https://github.com/rschu1ze)).
* 修复 protobuf 中 IPv6 编码错误的问题 [#49933](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/49933) ([Yakov Olkhovskiy](https://github.com/yakov-olkhovskiy)).
* 修复在 `ReplicatedMergeTree` 的 attach 线程中启动表时可能发生死锁的问题 [#50026](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50026) ([Antonio Andelic](https://github.com/antonio2368)).
* 修复 JIT 编译中 not equals NaN 的问题 [#50056](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50056) ([Maksim Kita](https://github.com/kitaisreal)).
* 修复 `multiIf` 在常量条件与 Nullable 参数组合下导致崩溃的问题 [#50123](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50123) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
* 修复 Keeper 在预处理请求时因异常而死锁的问题。[#50387](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50387) ([frinkr](https://github.com/frinkr)).
