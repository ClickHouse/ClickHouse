<div id="buildtestingpackaging-improvement">
  #### 构建/测试/打包改进
</div>

* 已在 [#48215](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/48215) 中回移：使用 sccache 替代 ccache，并使用 S3 作为缓存后端。 [#46240](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/46240) ([Mikhail f. Shiryaev](https://github.com/Felixoid)).
* 已在 [#48254](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/48254) 中回移：`clickhouse/clickhouse-keeper` 镜像过去只会以 `-alpine` 标签推送，例如 `latest-alpine`。按照 [https://github.com/ClickHouse/examples/pull/2](https://github.com/ClickHouse/examples/pull/2) 中的建议，现在也会以无后缀形式推送。 [#48236](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/48236) ([Mikhail f. Shiryaev](https://github.com/Felixoid)).
