<div id="clickhouse-release-v254322-stable-f23535c7722-fixme-as-compared-to-v254231-stable-e2ce00b6885">
  ### ClickHouse 发布 v25.4.3.22-稳定版本 (f23535c7722) FIXME，较 v25.4.2.31-稳定版本 (e2ce00b6885)
</div>

<div id="performance-improvement">
  #### 性能提升
</div>

* 已在 [#79924](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/79924) 中回移：防止 `LogSeriesLimiter` 在每次构造时都进行清理，避免在高并发场景下出现锁竞争和性能回退。[#79864](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/79864) ([filimonov](https://github.com/filimonov)) 。

<div id="improvement">
  #### 改进
</div>

* 已在 [#79904](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/79904) 中回移：新增 `access_control_improvements.enable_user_name_access_type` 设置。该设置可为用户/角色启用或禁用精确授权，此功能在 [https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/72246](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/72246) 中引入。如果你的集群中有版本早于 25.1 的副本，可能需要关闭此设置。[#79842](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/79842) ([pufit](https://github.com/pufit)).
* 已在 [#79934](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/79934) 中回移：`ASTSelectWithUnionQuery::clone()` 方法的正确实现现已将 `is_normalized` 字段也纳入考虑。这可能有助于解决 [#77569](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/77569)。[#79909](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/79909) ([Nikita Mikhaylov](https://github.com/nikitamikhaylov)).

<div id="bug-fix-user-visible-misbehavior-in-an-official-stable-release">
  #### 缺陷修复 (官方稳定版本中用户可见的异常行为)
</div>

* 已在 [#79820](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/79820) 中回移：修复从服务器应用 `async_insert` (通过 `apply_settings_from_server`) 的问题 (此前会导致客户端出现 `Unknown packet 11 from server` 错误) 。[#77578](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/77578) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* 已在 [#79785](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/79785) 中回移：修复可刷新materialized view 导致备份失效的问题。[#77893](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/77893) ([Michael Kolupaev](https://github.com/al13n321)).
* 已在 [#79741](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/79741) 中回移：修复 MergeTree 中 Wide parts 的动态子列发现过程中发生崩溃的问题。[#79466](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/79466) ([Pavel Kruglov](https://github.com/Avogar)).
* 已在 [#79585](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/79585) 中回移：仅对初始创建查询校验表名长度。对于次级创建操作，不进行此校验，以避免向后兼容性问题。[#79488](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/79488) ([Miсhael Stetsyuk](https://github.com/mstetsyuk)).
* 已在 [#79973](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/79973) 中回移：对查询 `CREATE DATABASE datalake ENGINE = DataLakeCatalog(\'http://catalog:8181\', \'admin\', \'password\')` 隐藏密码。[#79941](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/79941) ([Han Fei](https://github.com/hanfei1991)).
