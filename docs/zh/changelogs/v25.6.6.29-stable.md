<div id="bug-fix-user-visible-misbehavior-in-an-official-stable-release">
  #### 缺陷修复 (官方稳定版本中用户可见的异常行为)
</div>

* 已回移到 [#84395](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/84395)：修复 Dynamic 列在解析失败时的回滚问题。[#82169](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/82169) ([Pavel Kruglov](https://github.com/Avogar)).
* 已回移到 [#84671](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/84671)：修复在等值操作数类型不同或引用常量时，将过滤器合并到 JOIN 条件中的问题。修复了 [#83432](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/83432)。[#84145](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/84145) ([Dmitry Novik](https://github.com/novikd)).
* 已回移到 [#84558](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/84558)：修复由后台取消检查线程导致的死锁问题。[#84203](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/84203) ([Antonio Andelic](https://github.com/antonio2368)).
* 已回移到 [#84493](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/84493)：修复了 `array()` 函数中空元组构造错误的问题。此修复解决了 [#84202](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/84202)。[#84297](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/84297) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).
* 已回移到 [#84453](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/84453)：将 `zoutofmemory` 设为硬件错误，否则会抛出逻辑错误。参见 [https://github.com/clickhouse/clickhouse-core-incidents/issues/877](https://github.com/clickhouse/clickhouse-core-incidents/issues/877)。[#84420](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/84420) ([Han Fei](https://github.com/hanfei1991)).
* 已回移到 [#84652](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/84652)：修复 Keeper 更新日志中的乱序写入问题。此前，更新日志中可能存在正在进行的写入，但回滚可能导致目标文件被并发修改。这会导致日志不一致，并可能造成数据丢失。[#84434](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/84434) ([Antonio Andelic](https://github.com/antonio2368)).
* 已回移到 [#84627](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/84627)：修复在数据湖中按虚拟列裁剪文件的问题。[#84520](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/84520) ([Kseniia Sumarokova](https://github.com/kssenii)).
* 已回移到 [#84632](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/84632)：为保持兼容性，将 `allow_experimental_delta_kernel_rs` 在 25.5 之前的取值改为 `false`。[#84587](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/84587) ([Kseniia Sumarokova](https://github.com/kssenii)).
* 已回移到 [#84727](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/84727)：在 RefreshTask 中从 &#39;view&#39; 获取 zookeeper 时锁定 &#39;mutex&#39;。[#84699](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/84699) ([Tuan Pham Anh](https://github.com/tuanpach)).
