ThoughtSpot can connect to ClickHouse Cloud or on-premise deployment via its native ClickHouse connector.

<Step title="Gather your connection details" id="1-gather-your-connection-details">

<ConnectionDetails />

</Step>
<Step title="Create a ClickHouse connection in ThoughtSpot" id="2-create-a-clickhouse-connection-in-thoughtspot">

1. Navigate to **Data workspace** > **Connections**.
2. Click **+ Create connection**, select the **ClickHouse** tile, then click **Next**.
3. Enter a name for your connection, an optional description, and the ClickHouse connection details you gathered in the previous step, then click **Next**. For details on each attribute, see the [ClickHouse connection reference](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/connections-clickhouse-reference) in the ThoughtSpot documentation.
4. (Optional) To configure a query-level timeout, open the **Advanced Config** menu. For **Key**, enter `queryTimeoutSeconds`. For **Value**, enter the timeout in seconds. A maximum of 10 minutes is recommended.
5. (Optional) To provide additional key-value pairs, open the **Advanced Config** menu and enter a **Key** and **Value**. Click the plus sign (**+**) to add more pairs.

   <Note>
   Any key-value pairs that you enter must be defined in your ClickHouse data source. Key-value pairs are case-sensitive.
   </Note>

6. Select the tables and columns you want to use, then click **Create connection**. A message confirms the number of tables and columns added.

The connection now appears on the **Data workspace** > **Connections** page. You can search your ClickHouse data and run live queries on the linked tables in ThoughtSpot.
</Step>
</Steps>
