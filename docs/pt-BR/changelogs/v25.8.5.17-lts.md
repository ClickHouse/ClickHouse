<div id="bug-fix-user-visible-misbehavior-in-an-official-stable-release">

</div>

* Retroportado em [#87312](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/87312): Corrige tabelas KeeperMap criadas antes da 25.1 que deixavam dados no ZooKeeper após a consulta DROP. [#87112](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/87112) ([Nikolay Degterinsky](https://github.com/evillique)).
* Retroportado em [#87168](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/87168): Corrige a leitura de array com a subcoluna de tamanhos de array na lazy materialization. [#87139](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/87139) ([Pavel Kruglov](https://github.com/Avogar)).
* Retroportado em [#87394](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/87394): Corrige a contabilização incorreta das desalocações de dados temporários no rastreamento do limite `max_temporary_data_on_disk_size`, fecha [#87118](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/87118). [#87140](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/87140) ([JIaQi](https://github.com/JiaQiTang98)).
* Retroportado em [#87329](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/87329): Corrige um possível resultado incorreto de `EXISTS` não correlacionado. O problema foi introduzido com `execute_exists_as_scalar_subquery=1`, em [https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/85481](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/85481), e afeta `25.8`. Corrige [#86415](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/86415). [#87207](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/87207) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
* Retroportado em [#87437](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/87437): Corrige o `LOGICAL_ERROR` &quot;Too large size passed to allocator&quot; em `JOIN` devido à mistura de blocos const e non-const. [#87231](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/87231) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
