Anteriormente, o ClickHouse sempre retornava 0; isso foi corrigido. Corrige [#16605](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/16605). [#17006](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17006) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Retroportado em [#17432](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17432): Correção de bug na função fuzzBits, problema relacionado: [#16980](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/16980). [#17051](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17051) ([hexiaoting](https://github.com/hexiaoting)).
* Retroportado em [#17129](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17129): Corrige falha na consulta `CREATE TABLE ... AS some_table` quando `some_table` era criada com `AS table_function()`. Corrige [#16944](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/16944). [#17072](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17072) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).

Isso corrigiu [#17088](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17088). [#17135](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17135) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).
* Retroportado em [#17402](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17402): Corrige [#15235](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15235). Quando o clickhouse-copier lida com uma tabela sem partição, lança um erro de segfault. [#17248](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17248) ([Qi Chen](https://github.com/kaka11chen)).
* Retroportado em [#17409](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17409): Corrige a invalidação do índice Set quando há colunas const na subconsulta. Isso corrige [#17246](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17246). [#17249](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17249) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).

Corrige [#17228](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17228). [#17397](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17397) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
* Retroportado em [#17492](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17492): Corrige duplicatas após `DISTINCT`, que eram possíveis devido a uma otimização incorreta. Corrige [#17294](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17294). [#17296](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17296) ([li chengxiang](https://github.com/chengxianglibra)). [#17439](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17439) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
* Retroportado em [#17522](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17522): Corrige `ORDER BY` com a configuração `optimize_redundant_functions_in_order_by` habilitada. [#17471](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17471) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
* Retroportado em [#17535](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17535): Corrige um erro em que o tamanho do cache de marcas era subestimado pelo ClickHouse. Isso pode acontecer quando há muitos arquivos minúsculos com marcas. [#17496](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17496) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado em [#17627](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17627): Corrige o travamento da consulta ALTER quando a mutação correspondente foi interrompida em uma réplica diferente. Corrige [#16953](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/16953). [#17499](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17499) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado em [#17608](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17608): Quando o clickhouse-client é usado no modo interativo com consultas multilinha, o comentário de uma linha era estendido erroneamente até o fim da consulta. Isso corrige [#13654](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/13654). [#17565](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17565) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Retroportado em [#17729](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17729): Corrigido o erro `Function not implemented` ao executar a consulta `RENAME` no banco de dados `Atomic` com o ClickHouse em execução no Windows Subsystem for Linux. Corrige [#17661](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17661). [#17664](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17664) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
* Retroportado em [#17782](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17782): Corrigido o problema em que o ClickHouse não consegue retomar a conexão com servidores MySQL. [#17681](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17681) ([Alexander Kazakov](https://github.com/Akazz)).
* Retroportado em [#17814](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17814): Não restaura partes do WAL se `in_memory_parts_enable_wal` estiver desabilitado. [#17802](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/17802) ([detailyang](https://github.com/detailyang)).
