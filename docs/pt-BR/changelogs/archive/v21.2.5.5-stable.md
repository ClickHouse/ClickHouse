<div id="bug-fix">
  #### Correção de bug
</div>

* Backport em [#20574](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/20574): Corrige um crash que podia acontecer se um pacote desconhecido fosse recebido da consulta remove (introduzido em [#17868](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/17868)). [#20547](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/20547) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Backport em [#21006](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21006): Corrige o erro &#39;Empty task was returned from async task queue&#39; ao cancelar uma consulta. [#20881](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/20881) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Backport em [#21132](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21132): Corrige o comportamento em que `ALTER MODIFY COLUMN` criava uma mutação que sabidamente falharia. [#21007](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21007) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
* Backport em [#21251](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21251): - Bloqueia inserções paralelas em storage join. [#21009](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21009) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* Backport em [#21069](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21069): Era possível ocorrer acesso à memória fora dos limites ao formatar um valor do tipo `DateTime64` especificamente criado fora do intervalo. Isto fecha [#20494](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/20494). Isto fecha [#20543](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/20543). [#21023](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21023) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Backport em [#21160](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21160): Corrige `input_format_null_as_default` para que tenha efeito quando os tipos forem Nullable. Isto corrige [#21116](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21116). [#21121](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21121) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).
* Backport em [#21228](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21228): Corrige [#21112](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21112). Corrige um bug que podia causar duplicatas na consulta de inserção (se um dos callbacks chegasse um pouco tarde demais). [#21138](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21138) ([Kseniia Sumarokova](https://github.com/kssenii)).
* Backport em [#21276](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21276): Corrige um bug com `join_use_nulls` ao fazer join de `TOTALS` de subconsultas. Isto fecha [#19362](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/19362) e [#21137](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21137). [#21248](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21248) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
