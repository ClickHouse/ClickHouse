<div id="bug-fix">
  #### Correção de bug
</div>

* Backport em [#23818](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23818): Corrige falha inesperada quando `PREWHERE` e o filtro da política de linha estão ativos e o resultado é vazio. [#23763](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23763) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).
* Backport em [#23798](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23798): Corrige problema de timeout de conexão na bridge JDBC remota. Fecha [#9609](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/9609). [#23771](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23771) ([Maksim Kita](https://github.com/kitaisreal)).
* Backport em [#23815](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23815): Corrige o problema em que `CLEAR COLUMN` não funciona quando a coluna é referenciada por uma visão materializada. Fecha [#23764](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23764). [#23781](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23781) ([flynn](https://github.com/ucasfl)).
* Backport em [#23832](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23832): Corrige o erro `Can't initialize pipeline with empty pipe` em consultas com `GLOBAL IN/JOIN` e `use_hedged_requests`. Corrige [#23431](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23431). [#23805](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23805) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
* Backport em [#23927](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23927): Corrige a carga inicial do campo de atualização de chave complexa no HashedDictionary. Fecha [#23800](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/23800). [#23824](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/23824) ([Maksim Kita](https://github.com/kitaisreal)).
