* Retroportado em [#40160](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/40160): corrige o `HashMethodOneNumber`, que obtém um valor de chave incorreto quando a coluna é const. [#40020](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/40020) ([Duc Canh Le](https://github.com/canhld94)).

Corrige [#39896](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/39896). [#40095](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/40095) ([Jianmei Zhang](https://github.com/zhangjmruc)).

Fecha [#39987](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/39987). [#40194](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/40194) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Retroportado em [#40270](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/40270): corrige um possível segfault no formato de entrada CapnProto. Esse bug foi encontrado e enviado por meio do [programa](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/38986) de bug bounty do ClickHouse por *kiojj*.
