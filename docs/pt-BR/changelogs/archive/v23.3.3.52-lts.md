* Corrige erro de chave não encontrada em consultas com múltiplos StorageJoin [#49137](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/49137) ([vdimir](https://github.com/vdimir)).

* Corrige bug no DISTINCT [#49628](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/49628) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Corrige problema do msan em randomStringUTF8(<número ímpar>) [#49750](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/49750) ([Robert Schulze](https://github.com/rschu1ze)).
* Corrige `is_prefix` em OptimizeRegularExpression [#49919](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/49919) ([Han Fei](https://github.com/hanfei1991)).
* Corrige codificação de IPv6 no Protobuf [#49933](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/49933) ([Yakov Olkhovskiy](https://github.com/yakov-olkhovskiy)).
* Evita deadlock ao iniciar tabela na thread de attach do `ReplicatedMergeTree` [#50026](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50026) ([Antonio Andelic](https://github.com/antonio2368)).
* Corrige comparação de desigualdade com NaN na compilação JIT [#50056](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50056) ([Maksim Kita](https://github.com/kitaisreal)).
* Corrige crash com `multiIf` e condição constante com argumentos Nullable [#50123](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50123) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
* Corrige reconexão de sessão HTTPS quando o IP do host de destino é alterado [#50240](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50240) ([Aleksei Filatov](https://github.com/aalexfvk)).
* Corrige conversão de tipo de Date/Date32 para DateTime64 ao consultar com índice DateTime64 [#50280](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50280) ([Lucas Chang](https://github.com/lucas-tubi)).
* Corrige deadlock no Keeper ao ocorrer exceção durante o pré-processamento de requisições. [#50387](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50387) ([frinkr](https://github.com/frinkr)).
* Corrige constant folding incorreto [#50536](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50536) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Corrige contagem incorreta de linhas retornada por tabelas da família Log após TRUNCATE [#50585](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50585) ([flynn](https://github.com/ucasfl)).
* Corrige bug na mesclagem paralela de `uniqExact` [#50590](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50590) ([Nikita Taranov](https://github.com/nickitat)).
* Evita a leitura de todas as colunas da tabela direita em GLOBAL JOIN. [#50721](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50721) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
