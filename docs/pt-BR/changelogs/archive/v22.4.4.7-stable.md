* Retroportado em [#36524](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/36524): Queries com aliases dentro de operadores especiais retornavam erro de parsing (deixou de funcionar na 22.1). Exemplo: `SELECT substring('test' AS t, 1, 1)`. [#36167](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/36167) ([Maksim Kita](https://github.com/kitaisreal)).
* Retroportado em [#36673](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/36673): Corrige merges de partes no formato wide com tipo `Object`. [#36637](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/36637) ([Anton Popov](https://github.com/CurtizJ)).
