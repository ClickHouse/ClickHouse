[#64517](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/64517) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
* Retroportado em [#71600](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/71600): corrige o erro Invalid number of rows in Chunk with Variant column. [#71388](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/71388) ([Pavel Kruglov](https://github.com/Avogar)).
