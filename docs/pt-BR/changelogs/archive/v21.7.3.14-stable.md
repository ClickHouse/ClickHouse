<div id="bug-fix">
  #### Correção de bug
</div>

* Retroportado em [#26191](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26191): Corrige `sharding_key` de coluna sem função para `remote()` (antes, `select * from remote('127.1', system.one, dummy)` resultava no erro `Unknown column: dummy, there are only columns .`). [#25824](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/25824) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Retroportado em [#26109](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26109): Corrige um raro crash do servidor causado por `abort` no cliente ZooKeeper. Corrige [#25813](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/25813). [#26079](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26079) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado em [#26142](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26142): Corrige um possível crash em `pointInPolygon` se a configuração `validate_polygons` estiver desativada. [#26113](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26113) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Retroportado em [#26170](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26170): Corrige `joinGet` com colunas LowCarinality, fecha [#25993](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/25993). [#26118](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26118) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* Retroportado em [#26206](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26206): Corrige um possível crash se mais de uma expressão `untuple` for usada. [#26179](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26179) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Retroportado em [#26229](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26229): Remove a quebra de linha excessiva na coluna `thread_name` da tabela `system.stack_trace`. Isso corrige [#24124](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/24124). [#26210](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26210) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Corrige o lançamento de exceção ao iterar sobre um diretório remoto inexistente. [#26296](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26296) ([ianton-ru](https://github.com/ianton-ru)).
