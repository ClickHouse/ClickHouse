* Corrige a compatibilidade retroativa do hashing de tipos IP em funções de agregação [#50551](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/50551) ([Yakov Olkhovskiy](https://github.com/yakov-olkhovskiy)).

* Corrige a transferência de condições &#39;IN&#39; para PREWHERE [#51610](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/51610) ([Alexander Gololobov](https://github.com/davenger)).
* Corrige a leitura de uma coluna vazia em `parseSipHashKey` [#51804](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/51804) ([Nikita Taranov](https://github.com/nickitat)).
* Verifica o `refcount` em `RemoveManyObjectStorageOperation::finalize` em vez de em `execute` [#51954](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/51954) ([vdimir](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* Permite UDFs paramétricas [#51964](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/51964) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
