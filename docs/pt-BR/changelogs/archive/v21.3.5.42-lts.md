<div id="bug-fix">
  #### Correção de bug
</div>

* Retroportado em [#22335](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22335): Corrige a table function `clusterAllReplicas`, que retorna `_shard_num` incorreto. fecha [#21481](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21481). [#21498](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21498) ([flynn](https://github.com/ucasfl)).
* Retroportado em [#22319](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22319): Remove colunas desconhecidas da tabela associada no `where` para consultas a motores de banco de dados externos (MySQL, PostgreSQL). fecha [#14614](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14614), fecha [#19288](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/19288) (dup), fecha [#19645](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/19645) (dup). [#21640](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21640) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* Retroportado em [#22241](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22241): Corrige a análise de índice de scalar subquery. Isso corrige [#21717](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21717), introduzido em [https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/18896](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/18896). [#21766](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21766) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).
* Retroportado em [#22150](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22150): Melhora o tratamento de erros e o logging no WriteBufferFromS3. [#21836](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21836) ([Pavel Kovalenko](https://github.com/Jokser)).
* Retroportado em [#22468](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22468): Em casos raros, o merge de `CollapsingMergeTree` pode criar um grânulo com `index_granularity + 1` linhas. Por causa disso, a verificação interna adicionada em [#18928](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/18928) (afeta 21.2 e 21.3) pode falhar com o erro `Incomplete granules are not allowed while blocks are granules size`. Esse erro impedia o merge das partes. [#21976](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21976) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
* Retroportado em [#22203](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22203): Entrypoint do Docker: evita `chown` de `.` quando `LOG_PATH` está vazio. Fecha [#22100](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22100). [#22102](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22102) ([filimonov](https://github.com/filimonov)).
* Retroportado em [#22261](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22261): Desabilita `async_socket_for_remote`/`use_hedged_requests` para kernels Linux com bug. [#22109](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22109) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Retroportado em [#22281](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22281): Corrige a espera por consultas `OPTIMIZE` e `ALTER` em motores de tabela `ReplicatedMergeTree`. Agora, a consulta não ficará travada quando a tabela tiver sido desanexada ou reiniciada. [#22118](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22118) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado em [#22265](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22265): Corrige o nome do pool de background threads. [#22122](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22122) ([fastio](https://github.com/fastio)).
* Retroportado em [#22262](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22262): Corrige o erro `Invalid number of rows in Chunk` em `JOIN` com `TOTALS` e `arrayJoin`. Fecha [#19303](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/19303). [#22129](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22129) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* Retroportado em [#22316](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22316): Corrige o entrypoint do Docker caso `http_port` não esteja na config. [#22132](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22132) ([Ewout](https://github.com/devwout)).
* Retroportado em [#22313](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22313): Corrige o cancelamento de consulta com `use_hedged_requests=0` e `async_socket_for_remote=1`. [#22183](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22183) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Retroportado em [#22366](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22366): Agora o ClickHouse não lançará a exceção `LOGICAL_ERROR` ao tentar fazer mutação na parte já coberta. Corrige [#22013](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22013). [#22291](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22291) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado em [#22562](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22562): Não limita o tamanho dos fragmentos HTTP. Corrige [#21907](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21907). [#22322](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22322) ([Ivan](https://github.com/abyss7)).
* Retroportado em [#22531](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22531): Era possível ocorrer estouro de buffer (na leitura) no índice de texto completo `tokenbf_v1`. Os bytes excedentes não são usados, mas a operação de leitura pode levar a falha em casos raros. Isso fecha [#19233](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/19233). [#22421](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22421) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Retroportado em [#22463](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22463): Adiciona a contabilização de memória (ausente) nas rotinas de parsing em paralelo. Em versões anteriores, OOM era possível quando o conjunto de resultados continha blocos de dados muito grandes. Isso fecha [#22008](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22008). [#22425](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22425) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Retroportado em [#22507](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22507): Remove o socket do epoll antes de cancelar o receptor de pacotes em HedgedConnections para evitar uma possível condição de corrida. Espero que isso corrija [#22161](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22161). [#22443](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22443) ([Kruglov Pavel](https://github.com/Avogar)).
* Retroportado em [#22554](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22554): Corrige um bug no partial merge join com `LowCardinality`. Fecha [#22386](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22386), fecha [#22388](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22388). [#22510](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22510) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* Retroportado em [#22607](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22607): Corrige a desserialização de string vazia sem quebra de linha no fim do formato TSV. Isso fecha [#20244](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/20244). Solução alternativa possível sem atualizar a versão: definir `input_format_null_as_default` como zero. Era zero nas versões antigas. [#22527](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22527) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Backport em [#22576](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22576): Corrige UB ao desbloquear o rwlock do TinyLog na mesma thread. [#22560](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22560) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Backport em [#22651](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22651): Evita UB em *motores Log ao desbloquear o rwlock a partir de outra thread. [#22583](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22583) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Backport em [#22653](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22653): Tenta fazer flush do buffer de escrita apenas se ele estiver inicializado. Corrige segfault quando o cliente fecha a conexão muito cedo [#22579](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22579). [#22591](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22591) ([nvartolomei](https://github.com/nvartolomei)).
* Backport em [#22681](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22681): Corrige LOGICAL&#95;ERROR em Log com tipos Nested sem colunas na cláusula SELECT. [#22654](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/22654) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
