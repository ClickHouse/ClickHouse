<div id="bug-fix">
  #### Correção de bug
</div>

* Retroportado em [#21352](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21352): Corrige o número de threads para subconsultas escalares e subconsultas para índice (após [#19007](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/19007), uma única thread era sempre usada). Corrige [#20457](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/20457), [#20512](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/20512). [#20550](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/20550) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
* Retroportado em [#21262](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21262): corrige o fato de que os valores default&#95;replica&#95;path e default&#95;replica&#95;name são inúteis no motor Replicated(*)MergeTree quando ele precisa especificar outros parâmetros. [#21060](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21060) ([mxzlxy](https://github.com/mxzlxy)).
* Retroportado em [#21156](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21156): corrige um bug relacionado à conversão de Tuple para map. Fecha [#21029](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21029). [#21120](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21120) ([hexiaoting](https://github.com/hexiaoting)).
* Retroportado em [#21234](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21234): Agora, mutações são permitidas apenas para motores de tabela que as suportam (família MergeTree, Memory, MaterializedView). Outros motores relatarão um erro mais claro. Corrige [#21168](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21168). [#21183](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21183) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado em [#21427](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21427): Corrige uma falha em `EXPLAIN` para consulta com `UNION`. Corrige [#20876](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/20876), [#21170](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21170). [#21246](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21246) ([flynn](https://github.com/ucasfl)).
* Retroportado em [#21301](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21301): Corrige reconexões redundantes ao ZooKeeper e a possibilidade de duas sessões ativas para um único servidor ClickHouse. Ambos os problemas foram introduzidos em [#14678](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14678). [#21264](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21264) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado em [#21553](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21553): Agora, consultas `ALTER MODIFY COLUMN` afetarão corretamente alterações na chave de partição, índices de salto, TTLs e assim por diante. Corrige [#13675](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/13675). [#21334](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21334) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado em [#21380](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21380): Corrige o erro `Bad cast from type ... to DB::ColumnLowCardinality` ao inserir em uma tabela com coluna `LowCardinality` a partir do formato `Values`. Corrige [#21140](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21140). [#21357](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/21357) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
