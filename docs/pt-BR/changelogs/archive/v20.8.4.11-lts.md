</div>

[#15442](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/15442) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).

[#14783](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14783) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).
* Retroportado em [#15148](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15148): Corrigido um problema em que o servidor poderia travar na inicialização ao se comunicar com o ZooKeeper, caso os arquivos de configuração precisassem ser obtidos do ZK (usando a opção de inclusão `from_zk`). Corrige [#14814](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14814). [#14843](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14843) ([Alexander Kuzmenkov](https://github.com/akuzm)).

[#14879](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14879) ([Nikita Mikhaylov](https://github.com/nikitamikhaylov)).
* Retroportado em [#14955](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14955): Corrigido o erro `.metadata.tmp File exists` ao usar o engine de banco de dados `MaterializeMySQL`. [#14898](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14898) ([Winter Zhang](https://github.com/zhang2014)).

[#14975](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14975) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
