* Corrige o uso de buffers de leitura no StreamingFormatExecutor [#57438](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/57438) ([Kruglov Pavel](https://github.com/Avogar)).

* Desabilita system.kafka&#95;consumers por padrão (devido a possível vazamento de memória em tempo de execução) [#57822](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/57822) ([Azat Khuzhin](https://github.com/azat)).
* Corrige acesso inválido à memória em BLAKE3 (Rust) [#57876](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/57876) ([Raúl Marín](https://github.com/Algunenano)).
* Normaliza nomes de funções em CREATE INDEX [#57906](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/57906) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
* Corrige pré-processamento inválido no Keeper [#58069](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/58069) ([Antonio Andelic](https://github.com/antonio2368)).
