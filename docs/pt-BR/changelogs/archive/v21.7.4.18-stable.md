* Retroportado em [#26297](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26297): Corrigido `sequence_id` incorreto em pacotes do protocolo MySQL enviados pelo ClickHouse em caso de exceção durante a execução da consulta. Isso pode fazer o cliente MySQL redefinir a conexão com o servidor ClickHouse. Corrige [#21184](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/21184). [#26051](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26051) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
* Retroportado em [#26356](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26356): Corrige erro lógico em join com totais; fecha [#26017](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26017). [#26250](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26250) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* Retroportado em [#26359](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26359): Corrigido um bug raro na recuperação de réplica perdida que pode fazer com que as réplicas divirjam. [#26321](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26321) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
* Retroportado em [#26419](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26419): Corrige possível falha ao fazer login como um usuário removido. Este PR corrige [#26073](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26073). [#26363](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26363) ([Vitaly Baranov](https://github.com/vitlibar)).
* Retroportado em [#26413](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26413): Corrige fluxo infinito de blocks sem join em `partial_merge_join`; fecha [#26325](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26325). [#26374](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26374) ([Vladimir C](https://github.com/vdimir)).
* Retroportado em [#26447](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/26447): Corrige algumas falhas do msan encontradas por fuzzing. Corrige [#22517](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22517). [#26428](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/26428) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
