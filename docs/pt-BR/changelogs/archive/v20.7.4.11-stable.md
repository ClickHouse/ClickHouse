<div id="improvement">
  #### Melhoria
</div>

[#15442](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/15442) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).



<div id="bug-fix">
  #### Correção de Bug
</div>

* Retroportado em [#15019](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15019): Corrigida a ordem de classificação incorreta da coluna `Nullable`. Corrige [#14344](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14344). [#14495](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14495) ([Nikita Mikhaylov](https://github.com/nikitamikhaylov)).

[#14783](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14783) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).
* Retroportado em [#15149](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15149): Corrigido um problema em que o servidor poderia travar na inicialização ao se comunicar com o ZooKeeper, caso os arquivos de configuração precisassem ser obtidos do ZK (usando a opção de inclusão `from_zk`). Corrige [#14814](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14814). [#14843](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14843) ([Alexander Kuzmenkov](https://github.com/akuzm)).
* Retroportado em [#15252](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15252): Corrigido segfault no CacheDictionary [#14837](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14837). [#14879](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14879) ([Nikita Mikhaylov](https://github.com/nikitamikhaylov)).
* Retroportado em [#14988](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14988): Publicadas as frequências de CPU por núcleo lógico em `system.asynchronous_metrics`. Corrige [#14923](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14923). [#14924](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14924) ([Alexander Kuzmenkov](https://github.com/akuzm)).
* Retroportado em [#14970](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14970): Correção para que o pushdown de predicado funcione quando a subconsulta contém a função finalizeAggregation. Corrige [#14847](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14847). [#14937](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14937) ([filimonov](https://github.com/filimonov)).
* Retroportado em [#15054](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15054): Agora as configurações `number_of_free_entries_in_pool_to_execute_mutation` e `number_of_free_entries_in_pool_to_lower_max_size_of_merge` podem ser iguais a `background_pool_size`. [#14975](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/14975) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado em [#15078](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15078): Corrigido crash em RIGHT ou FULL JOIN com join_algorithm='auto' quando o limite de memória é excedido e é necessário substituir HashJoin por MergeJoin. [#15002](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/15002) ([Artem Zuikov](https://github.com/4ertus2)).
* Retroportado em [#15103](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15103): Corrigido o erro `Cannot rename ... errno: 22, strerror: Invalid argument` na execução de consultas DDL no banco de dados Atomic ao executar o clickhouse-server no Docker no Mac OS. [#15024](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/15024) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
* Retroportado em [#15058](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15058): Se a função `bar` fosse chamada com argumentos especialmente elaborados, era possível ocorrer estouro de buffer. Fecha [#13926](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/13926). [#15028](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/15028) ([Alexey Milovidov](https://github.com/alexey-milovidov)).
* Retroportado em [#15051](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15051): Já utilizamos comparação com preenchimento entre String e FixedString (https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/blob/master/src/Functions/FunctionsComparison.h#L333). Este PR aplica a mesma lógica à comparação de campos, corrigindo o uso de FixedString como chaves primárias. Corrige [#14908](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/14908). [#15033](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/15033) ([Amos Bird](https://github.com/amosbird)).
* Retroportado em [#15140](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15140): Corrige `Data compressed with different methods` em `join_algorithm='auto'`. Mantém LowCardinality como tipo para a chave de join da tabela esquerda em `join_algorithm='partial_merge'`. [#15088](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/15088) ([Artem Zuikov](https://github.com/4ertus2)).
* Retroportado em [#15221](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15221): Corrigido bug no engine de tabela `Buffer` que impedia a inserção de dados com nova estrutura no `Buffer` após uma consulta `ALTER`. Corrige [#15117](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15117). [#15192](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/15192) ([alesapin](https://github.com/alesapin)).
* Retroportado em [#15406](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/15406): Corrigido crash de instância ao usar joinGet com tipos LowCardinality.
