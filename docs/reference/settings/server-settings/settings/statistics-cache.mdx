These settings configure the ClickHouse server and are autogenerated from ClickHouse source.

## statistics_cache_policy {#statistics_cache_policy}

<SettingsInfoBlock type="String" default_value="SLRU" changeable_without_restart="No" />Statistics cache eviction policy name. Possible values: `LRU`, `SLRU`, `SIEVE`.

## statistics_cache_prewarm_ratio {#statistics_cache_prewarm_ratio}

<SettingsInfoBlock type="Double" default_value="0.95" changeable_without_restart="No" />The ratio of total size of the statistics cache to fill during prewarm.

## statistics_cache_size {#statistics_cache_size}

<SettingsInfoBlock type="UInt64" default_value="1073741824" changeable_without_restart="Yes" />Maximum size of the cache of column statistics of data parts of MergeTree family of tables. The statistics are cached per data part and column, loaded on demand, and merged on the fly for every query.

## statistics_cache_size_ratio {#statistics_cache_size_ratio}

<SettingsInfoBlock type="Double" default_value="0.5" changeable_without_restart="No" />The size of the protected queue (in case of SLRU policy) in the statistics cache relative to the cache's total size.
