[#63122](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/63122) ([Alexander Gololobov](https://github.com/davenger)).

[#63126](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/63126) ([Alexander Gololobov](https://github.com/davenger)).
