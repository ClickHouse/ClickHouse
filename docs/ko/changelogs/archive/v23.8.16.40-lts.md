* [#66449](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/66449)에 백포트됨: ZooKeeper 클라이언트의 버그를 수정했습니다. [#66140](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/66140) ([Alexander Tokmakov](https://github.com/tavplubix)).
