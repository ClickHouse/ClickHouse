[#44709](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/44709)를 해결합니다. [#44875](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/pull/44875) ([Nikolai Kochetov](https://github.com/KochetovNicolai)).
