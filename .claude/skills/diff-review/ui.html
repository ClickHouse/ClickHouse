Reviewing

Click the + over a line number, or drag across several, to comment. Every keystroke is written to , so nothing is lost to a closed tab or a killed server — the bar only speaks up when a write fails. Send round N hands the open comments over and leaves the review up, so you can keep reading while they are worked on.

The sidebar's Comments tab lists every comment — amber with Claude, green addressed, with the answer under it. On an addressed one, reply asks a follow-up on the same lines and hide takes it off the page for good; both are on the comment in the diff too. Any saved comment folds away to its first line — click its header, or fold at the end of it.

Ending the review stops the server. Asking Claude to close it does the same, so this is only here for when you would rather not.
